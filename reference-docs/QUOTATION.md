**Prepared for:** Oyecouture Bespoke · **Date:** 19 July 2026 · **Valid for:** 30 days

## 1. What we're building

A complete, custom-built online store on your existing domain, implementing the approved design:

| # | Deliverable |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Storefront** — Home/Shop, Suits, Trad Attires, Casuals (Men & Women), About; filterable & sortable product grid; product pages with image galleries and size selection; fully mobile-optimised |
| 2 | **Product search** — instant search from the navigation bar across names, categories and descriptions |
| 3 | **Bespoke order flow (WhatsApp-first)** — for bespoke pieces (suits, agbada, aso-oke, kaftans), the customer confirms their order straight to your WhatsApp with their order number. **Payment happens only after measurements, fabric and delivery are agreed** — you simply share the order's secure payment link in the chat and the customer pays online. Customers who already know their measurements can submit them in a structured form, and you receive them with the order |
| 4 | **Ready-to-wear checkout** — sized items (shirts, trousers, knitwear) go through a normal cart → checkout → pay-now flow with delivery address and configurable delivery fees (Lagos / Nigeria / International) |
| 5 | **Online payments** — Nomba (card, bank transfer, USSD; NGN & USD cards), engineered with a backup payment provider path so payments stay up even if one provider has an outage |
| 6 | **Order confirmations on email and WhatsApp** — every order and every payment triggers an automatic email to the customer and to you; every WhatsApp hand-off carries the order number so the conversation and the order stay linked |
| 7 | **Customer-care pages** — Size Guide, Shipping & Delivery, Returns & Exchanges, Contact, Book-a-Fitting request form, Terms & Privacy |
| 8 | **Launch** — deployment on your domain with HTTPS, SEO setup (Google-indexable product pages), analytics, and 2 weeks post-launch support |
